Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-25

FillKaro (“the extension”) is a Chrome extension by Kadir Laskar that fills web forms with details you save.

What data the extension handles

Profile details you type in Options (for example name, parents' names, date of birth, contact details, address, education, and — only if you enable it — ID numbers), your settings, and field rules you teach it for specific sites.

Where it is stored

Only on your device, in Chrome's extension storage (chrome.storage.local). It is not synced and not sent anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. The extension makes no network requests, has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share data.

Permissions

  • activeTab and scripting: to fill the form on the tab you are using, only when you click Fill, use the shortcut or the right-click menu.
  • storage: to keep your profiles and settings on your device.
  • contextMenus: for the right-click “Fill” and “Teach this field” options.

Sensitive fields

The extension never fills password, OTP, captcha or payment fields and never submits forms. ID numbers are off by default and masked in the interface.

Deleting your data

Delete profiles in Options, or remove the extension to delete everything it stored.
